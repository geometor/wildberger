geometor.wildberger.catalog
===========================

.. py:module:: geometor.wildberger.catalog

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Master catalog of the 28 Rational Trigonometry & Universal Geometry Principles.

   Organizes the mathematical principles of Rational Trigonometry and Universal
   Geometry into four parts (`W.01` through `W.28`), with constructive signatures,
   original GEOMETOR synthesis prose, exact LaTeX identities, cross-repository
   canon mappings, and constructive dependency DAG links.

.. py:class:: PrincipleSpec

   Specification of a single Principle Dossier (`W.01`..`W.28`).

   .. attribute:: num

      Integer order `1..28`.

   .. attribute:: code

      Canonical identifier `'W.01'`..`'W.28'`.

   .. attribute:: slug

      Directory slug `'w-01'`..`'w-28'`.

   .. attribute:: part

      Four-part thematic division name.

   .. attribute:: category

      Sub-domain category label.

   .. attribute:: tags

      Tuple of index tags.

   .. attribute:: title

      Principle title.

   .. attribute:: subtitle

      Concise descriptive subtitle.

   .. attribute:: enunciation

      Formal mathematical statement for `.. enunciation::`.

   .. attribute:: signature_given

      Tuple of `given` declarations for `.. signature::`.

   .. attribute:: signature_steps

      Tuple of constructive/algebraic steps for `.. signature::`.

   .. attribute:: synthesis

      Tuple of original GEOMETOR synthesis paragraphs (RST).

   .. attribute:: math_blocks

      Tuple of LaTeX display math strings for `.. math::`.

   .. attribute:: canon_links

      Tuple of `(label, rst_value)` cross-repository canon rows.

   .. attribute:: depends_on

      Tuple of upstream `'W.NN'` codes in the dependency DAG.
