Snellius
========

.. _snellius:

Willebrord Snellius (1580–1626)
-------------------------------

Dutch astronomer and geodesist who pioneered triangulation resection (1615, :ref:`W.25 <w-25>`) and the law of refraction (:math:`n_1^2 s_1 = n_2^2 s_2`, :ref:`W.26 <w-26>`).
