Conway
======

.. _conway:

John Horton Conway (1937–2020)
------------------------------

Introduced Conway triangle notation (:math:`S_A, S_B, S_C`), matching the quadratic Cross Law numerators :math:`(Q_j + Q_k - Q_i)/2`.
