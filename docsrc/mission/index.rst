=======
Mission
=======

``geometor.wildberger`` fulfills a foundational algebraic mission within the
**GEOMETOR** ecosystem:

1. **Unite Constructive Geometry with Rational Trigonometry**: Since its
   inception, ``geometor.model`` has represented points, lines, and circles in
   exact ``SymPy`` symbolic algebra rather than floating-point approximations.
   :person:`Norman J. Wildberger <wildberger>`'s *Divine Proportions*
   (:cite:`Wildberger2005`) reveals *why* geometry wants to be polynomial:
   replacing distance and angle with :term:`quadrance` and :term:`spread`
   eliminates unnecessary radicals and transcendental functions from the laws of
   trigonometry.
2. **Demonstrate Universal & Chromogeometric Laws Symbolically**: Every
   principle from :ref:`W.01 <w-01>` through :ref:`W.28 <w-28>` is backed by
   multivariate ``SymPy`` polynomial certificates (valid over any field of
   characteristic :math:`\ne 2`, including finite fields :math:`\mathbb{F}_p`),
   exact ``geometor.model`` constructions, and Explorer-styled interactive SVG
   diagrams.
3. **Bridge Ancient Canon and Modern Computation**: Connect :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s
   *Elements* (``geometor/euclid``, ``geometor/elements``), :person:`Pappus of Alexandria <pappus>`'s
   projective cross-ratios (``geometor/pappus``), :person:`Luca Pacioli <pacioli>`'s
   golden polyhedra (``geometor/pacioli``), and ``geometor/fundamentals`` to
   universal algebraic geometry and **Blue / Red / Green** :term:`Chromogeometry`.
