============================================================
Initial 28-Principle Rational Trigonometry & Universal Codex
============================================================

Launched ``geometor.wildberger`` v0.1.0, synthesizing the foundational and
advanced principles of :person:`Norman J. Wildberger <wildberger>`'s *Divine
Proportions: Rational Trigonometry to Universal Geometry* (:cite:`Wildberger2005`)
and *Chromogeometry* (:cite:`Wildberger2008`) into a 28-Principle executable
codex (:ref:`W.01 <w-01>` through :ref:`W.28 <w-28>`).

Highlights
----------

- **Exact Symbolic Engine** (:mod:`geometor.wildberger.invariants`): Implemented
  quadrance, spread, cross, twist, signed turn, Archimedes' function, quadrea,
  Triple/Quadruple Quad & Spread functions, Euler's four-point function, spread
  and cross polynomials, 6-proportion Taylor conics, 3D dihedral spreads,
  rational polar coordinates :math:`[s, Q]`, and finite-field :math:`\mathbb{F}_p`
  metric evaluation.
- **18 Multivariate SymPy Proofs** (:mod:`geometor.wildberger.proofs`): Proved
  the Five Main Laws, Brahmagupta & Ptolemy's cyclic theorems, Euler line and
  Nine-Point Circle over arbitrary fields (:math:`\operatorname{char}\mathbb{F}\ne 2`),
  Chromogeometry's Nine-Point Midpoint Theorem (:math:`N_B = \tfrac{1}{2}(C_R + C_G)`),
  parabolic optical reflection without derivatives, and the cancellation of
  :math:`\sqrt{5}` in all five Platonic solid dihedral spreads
  (:math:`8/9, 1, 8/9, 4/9, 4/5`).
- **28 Interactive Constructions**: Built exact ``geometor.model.Model``
  dossiers with zero floating-point shortcuts and interactive dark-canvas SVGs.
