W.08 Law III: The Spread Law & Extended Circumquadrance Law¶

Exact Rational Proportionality of Vertex Spreads and Opposite Quadrances

In any non-null triangle A_1 A_2 A_3 with side quadrances Q_1, Q_2, Q_3, vertex spreads s_1, s_2, s_3, quadrea A, and circumquadrance K, the ratios of spread to opposite quadrance are equal: s_1 / Q_1 = s_2 / Q_2 = s_3 / Q_3 = A / (4 * Q_1 * Q_2 * Q_3) = 1 / (4 * K).

A_1 : Point A_2 : Point A_3 : Point ===== t = polygon(A_1, A_2, A_3) F_1 = foot(A_1, line(A_2, A_3)) F_2 = foot(A_2, line(A_1, A_3)) C = circumcenter(A_1, A_2, A_3) c = circle(C, A_1)

Mathematical Synthesis¶

Dropping altitudes to feet F1 and F2 in triangle t gives two right-triangle expressions for the altitude quadrances: \(Q(A_1, F_1) = Q_3 s_2\) and \(Q(A_2, F_2) = Q_3 s_1\), while matching \(Q_2 s_3 = Q_3 s_2\) immediately yields the Spread Law \(s_1 / Q_1 = s_2 / Q_2 = s_3 / Q_3\) (Wildberger2005)—replacing the transcendental Law of Sines with pure field division!

Connecting the Spread Law to the circumcenter C and circumcircle c produces the Extended Spread Law: every ratio \(s_i / Q_i\) equals \(1 / (4 K)\), where \(K = Q(C, A_i) = Q_1 Q_2 Q_3 / \mathcal{A}\) is the circumquadrance—the exact quadratic counterpart of the classical \(a / \sin A = 2 R\) identity.

Key Algebraic Identities¶

\[\frac{s_1}{Q_1} = \frac{s_2}{Q_2} = \frac{s_3}{Q_3} = \frac{\mathcal{A}}{4 Q_1 Q_2 Q_3} = \frac{1}{4 K}\]
\[K = \frac{Q_1 Q_2 Q_3}{\mathcal{A}}, \qquad \mathcal{A} = 64 K^2 s_1 s_2 s_3\]
