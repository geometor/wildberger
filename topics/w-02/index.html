W.02 Lineations, Central Rotations & Reflections in Lines¶

Square-Root-Free Point and Line Reflections Across Arbitrary Non-Null Axes

For any non-null line l = <a : b : c> over a field F (char F != 2), the reflection sigma_l maps every point A = [x, y] and every line m = <a_2 : b_2 : c_2> to exact rational images whose denominators depend only on the quadratic norm a^2 + b^2, without radicals or angles.

P_1 : Point P_2 : Point A : Point B : Point ===== l = line(P_1, P_2) F = foot(A, l) A_r = reflect_point(A, l) m = line(A, B) m_r = reflect_line(m, l)

Mathematical Synthesis¶

Classical orthogonal reflection across an oblique axis typically invokes unit normal vectors containing \(\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) or trigonometric double-angle matrices. In Universal Geometry (Wildberger2005, SnapperTroyer1971), because the foot F of the altitude from A to l is rational in \((a, b, c)\), the reflected point Ar \(= 2 F - A\) is always rational over the ground field \(F\)!

Even more remarkably, reflecting an entire line m across l produces the reflected line mr \(= \Sigma_l(m)\) via homogeneous quadratic polynomials in \((a, b)\). Iterating line reflections generates cyclic reflection sequences that directly govern spread polynomials and regular star symmetries over both \(\mathbb{Q}\) and finite fields.

Key Algebraic Identities¶

\[\sigma_\ell([x, y]) = \left[\frac{(b^2 - a^2)x - 2 a b y - 2 a c}{a^2 + b^2},\; \frac{-2 a b x + (a^2 - b^2)y - 2 b c}{a^2 + b^2}\right]\]
\[\Sigma_\ell(\langle a_2 : b_2 : c_2 \rangle) = \langle (a^2 - b^2)a_2 + 2 a b b_2 :\; 2 a b a_2 - (a^2 - b^2)b_2 :\; 2 c(a a_2 + b b_2) - (a^2 + b^2)c_2 \rangle\]
