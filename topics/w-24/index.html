W.24 Exact Triangle Solvers & Surveying Triangulation¶

Polynomial QQQ/QQS/QSS/SQQ Solvers, Inaccessible Heights, and Regiomontanus’ Extremum

Every classical triangle specification (QQQ, QQS, QSS, SQQ, and SSS up to scaling) reduces to linear or quadratic equations in quadrances and spreads with exact discriminant branching; applied to surveying, inaccessible heights and Regiomontanus’ maximum-spread viewing problem resolve by pure rational algebra without trigonometric tables.

A : Point B : Point D_foot : Point C_top : Point ===== l_ground = line(A, D_foot) seg_tower = segment(D_foot, C_top) t_surv = polygon(A, B, C_top) P_obs = regiomontanus_optimal_point(D_foot, a, b)

Mathematical Synthesis¶

Classical triangle resolution (SSS, SAS, ASA, SSA) alternates awkwardly between inverse cosines, inverse sines, and tangent half-angle formulas, accumulating floating-point rounding at every step. In Rational Trigonometry (Wildberger2005), QQQ and QSS are purely rational in one step, while SQQ (Cross Law quadratic in \(Q_3\)), QQS (Spread Law + Cross Law), and SSS (proportional quadrances \(Q_1 : Q_2 : Q_3 = s_1 : s_2 : s_3\) once \(s_3\) solves the Triple Spread Formula) require at most a single quadratic root!

In field surveying, measuring horizontal baseline \(Q(A, B)\) along lground and the elevation spreads from A and B to tower top Ctop above base Dfoot (segtower, triangle tsurv) determines the vertical quadrance \(Q(D_{\text{foot}}, C_{\text{top}})\) by one rational formula. Likewise, Regiomontanus’ 1471 problem—maximizing the viewing spread of a vertical statue from \(y = a\) to \(y = b\)—completes the square without derivatives to yield optimal quadrance \(Q(D_{\text{foot}}, P_{\text{obs}}) = a b\) at Pobs with maximum spread \((b - a)^2 / (a + b)^2\)!

Key Algebraic Identities¶

\[\text{SQQ:}\quad (Q_3 - (Q_1 + Q_2))^2 - 4 Q_1 Q_2 (1 - s_3) = \mathcal{A}(Q_1, Q_2, Q_3) - 4 Q_1 Q_2 s_3 = 0\]
\[\text{Regiomontanus }(0 < a < b):\quad x_{\text{opt}}^2 = a b, \qquad s_{\max} = \frac{(b - a)^2}{(a + b)^2}\]
