W.17 Circle Geometry: Subtended Spreads, Secant Products & Rational Points¶

Thales’ Right-Diameter Law, Inscribed Spread s_2(s), and Stereographic Parametrization

On a circle Q(X, O_c) = K over F, a diameter subtends spread 1 at every other circle point (Thales), any chord P_1 P_2 subtends constant spread s_3 at the circumference with Q(P_1, P_2) = 4*K*s_3 and central spread s(O_c P_1, O_c P_2) = S_2(s_3) = 4*s_3*(1 - s_3), and any external or internal point A_0 has invariant secant quadrance product Q(A_0, P_1)*Q(A_0, P_2) = (Q(A_0, O_c) - K)^2.

O_c : Point P_1 : Point P_2 : Point B : Point A_0 : Point ===== c = circle(O_c, P_1) l_sec1 = line(A_0, P_1) l_sec2 = line(A_0, B) Q_1 = meet_other(c, l_sec1, P_1) Q_2 = meet_other(c, l_sec2, B)

Mathematical Synthesis¶

Classical circle theorems suffer from case distinctions between acute and obtuse inscribed angles (\(\theta\) vs. \(\pi - \theta\)). Because supplementary rays define the same line, every point B on circle c (centered at Oc) subtends the exact same spread \(s(B P_1, B P_2) = Q(P_1, P_2) / (4 K)\) on chord \(P_1 P_2\), regardless of which arc B inhabits (Wildberger2005)! And the central spread at Oc is governed by the second spread polynomial \(S_2(s_3) = 4 s_3 (1 - s_3)\).

Similarly, Euclid’s intersecting chords/secants theorem (III.35, III.36) becomes the Equal Products Theorem: lines lsec1 and lsec2 through A0 cut c at (P1, Q1) and (B, Q2) with invariant quadrance product \((Q(A_0, O_c) - K)^2\). Projecting from \([-1, 0]\) parametrizes the unit circle by \(\left[\frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2}, \frac{2 t}{1 + t^2}\right]\), generating all Pythagorean triples over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and counting \(p \mp 1\) points on circles over \(\mathbb{F}_p\).

Key Algebraic Identities¶

\[s(B P_1, B P_2) = \frac{Q(P_1, P_2)}{4 K} = s_3, \qquad s(O_c P_1, O_c P_2) = S_2(s_3) = 4 s_3 (1 - s_3)\]
\[Q(A_0, P_1)\, Q(A_0, Q_1) = Q(A_0, B)\, Q(A_0, Q_2) = \big(Q(A_0, O_c) - K\big)^2, \qquad U(t) = \left[\frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2},\; \frac{2 t}{1 + t^2}\right]\]
