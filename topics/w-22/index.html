W.22 Projective Cross-Ratio, Harmonic Pencils & The Pappus Bridge¶

Exact Equivalence of Line-Pencil Spread Cross-Ratio and Point-Range Quadrance Cross-Ratio

If four concurrent lines r_1, r_2, r_3, r_4 through O_p meet a transversal l_a at collinear points A_1, A_2, A_3, A_4 and another transversal l_b at B_1, B_2, B_3, B_4, then the squared cross-ratio in quadrances (Q(A_1, A_3)*Q(A_2, A_4)) / (Q(A_1, A_4)*Q(A_2, A_3)) equals the pencil spread cross-ratio (s_13 * s_24) / (s_14 * s_23) and is invariant across all transversals.

O_p : Point A_1 : Point A_2 : Point B_1 : Point B_2 : Point ===== l_a = line(A_1, A_2) l_b = line(B_1, B_2) r_1 = line(O_p, A_1) r_2 = line(O_p, A_2) r_3 = line(O_p, A_3) r_4 = line(O_p, A_4) B_3 = meet(r_3, l_b) B_4 = meet(r_4, l_b)

Mathematical Synthesis¶

Pappus of Alexandria discovered that the cross-ratio of four collinear points is preserved under central projection (CoxeterGreitzer1967). In Rational Trigonometry (Wildberger2005), squaring the classical signed cross-ratio replaces signed lengths with quadrance along transversals la (A1, A2, A3, A4) and lb (B1, B2, B3, B4), and replaces directed sine ratios in the pencil of rays r1, r2, r3, r4 through Op with pairwise spread!

Applying the Spread Law to the four sub-triangles at apex Op cancels all radial quadrances \(Q(O_p, A_i)\) and transversal spreads \(s(r_i, \ell_a)\) in a single stroke, proving \(R_Q(A_1, A_2; A_3, A_4) = R_s(r_1, r_2; r_3, r_4) = R_Q(B_1, B_2; B_3, B_4)\). When the signed cross-ratio is \(-1\) (harmonic conjugates), its quadrance cross-ratio is identically \((-1)^2 = 1\), directly uniting geometor/wildberger, geometor/pappus, and F.25 Harmonic Conjugates!

Key Algebraic Identities¶

\[(A_1, A_2; A_3, A_4)_Q = \frac{Q(A_1, A_3)\, Q(A_2, A_4)}{Q(A_1, A_4)\, Q(A_2, A_3)} = \frac{s(r_1, r_3)\, s(r_2, r_4)}{s(r_1, r_4)\, s(r_2, r_3)} = (B_1, B_2; B_3, B_4)_Q\]
\[\text{Harmonic Range } (A_1, A_2; A_3, A_4) = -1 \implies Q(A_1, A_3)\, Q(A_2, A_4) = Q(A_1, A_4)\, Q(A_2, A_3)\]
