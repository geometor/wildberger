geometor.wildberger.proofs¶

Symbolic SymPy theorem provers for Rational Trigonometry and Universal Geometry.

Every prover constructs an algebraic identity in general symbolic indeterminates (or over algebraic number fields Q(phi) and finite fields GF(p)), simplifies the difference LHS - RHS to exact polynomial 0 without floating-point shortcuts, and returns a structured ProofCertificate.

geometor.wildberger.proofs.prove_kepler_golden_triangle() → ProofCertificate¶

Prove exact quadrance and spread identities of Kepler’s Golden Right Triangle.

In classical geometry, Kepler’s triangle has irrational nested-radical side lengths (1, sqrt(phi), phi). In Rational Trigonometry, its quadrances are purely powers of phi (Q = (1, phi, phi^2)) and its spreads are (1/phi^2, 1/phi, 1) = (2 - phi, phi - 1, 1)!

Verified ProofCertificate.

geometor.wildberger.proofs.prove_chromogeometry_theorems() → ProofCertificate¶

Prove Wildberger’s Chromogeometry Parallelogram & Medial Nine-Point Theorem symbolically.

Proves for ANY planar triangle A1 A2 A3 (via translational frame A_3 = [0, 0]) across Blue (dx^2 + dy^2), Red (dx^2 - dy^2), and Green (2*dx*dy) geometries: 1. N_B = (C_R + C_G) / 2, N_R = (C_B + C_G) / 2, N_G = (C_B + C_R) / 2! 2. C_B + O_B = C_R + C_G (so C_B C_R O_B C_G is always a parallelogram)! 3. All three Euler lines C_B O_B, C_R O_R, C_G O_G concur at Centroid G!

Returns:

Verified ProofCertificate.

geometor.wildberger.proofs.prove_platonic_dihedral_spreads() → ProofCertificate¶

Prove from 3D coordinates and 2D sections that all 5 Platonic solids have purely rational dihedral spreads!

Exact Face (Dihedral) Spreads (Chapter 26): - Regular Tetrahedron: S = 8/9 - Cube (Hexahedron): S = 1 - Regular Octahedron: S = 8/9 - Regular Icosahedron: S = 4/9 - Regular Dodecahedron: S = 4/5

Even for the Golden Ratio polyhedra (Icosahedron and Dodecahedron), sqrt(5) cancels completely in the quadratic dihedral spread! Also proves 3D Pythagoras (De Gua’s Theorem in Quadreas): A_ABC = A_OAB + A_OBC + A_OCA.

Verified ProofCertificate.
